# Stratum Venture Intelligence (Origin)

> **Institutional-Grade AI Startup Evaluator & Strategic Intelligence Platform**

Stratum (Origin) is an advanced venture intelligence platform designed to stress-test early-stage business hypotheses, evaluate defensibility moats, analyze bottom-up market sizing, and forecast unit economics curves using foundational strategic frameworks.

---

## 🏛 Core Frameworks & Architecture
- **NVIDIA NIM AI Backbone:** Powered by **Nemotron-3 Ultra 550B** reasoning models with streaming thought synthesis.
- **6 Core Business Pillars Engine:**
  1. **Market Size:** TAM, SAM, SOM, bottom-up account volumes, ACV, and CAGR.
  2. **Customer Segments:** Enterprise, Mid-Market, and SMB segmentation with ICP personas.
  3. **Business Model:** Monetization flywheel, usage vs. subscription mix, net revenue expansion.
  4. **Unit Economics:** CAC, LTV, Payback velocity, and Gross Margin profiles.
  5. **USP & Defensibility Moat:** Hamilton Helmer’s 7 Powers (Switching Costs, Network Effects, Counter-Positioning, etc.).
  6. **Branding & Positioning:** Brand archetype, distinctiveness index, and strategic positioning.
- **Medieval Roman Torn-Paper Aesthetic:** Bespoke deckled parchment visual styling, wax seal indicators, and smooth emergence transitions.
